Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026.

What OmniStore reads

  • Your device: drive sizes, free space, disk type and health, the size of cloud app folders, the Recycle Bin, temporary files and iPhone or iPad backups stored on the device.
  • iCloud: after you sign in on Apple's own page, your storage plan, total usage, usage by category and the list of Apple devices on your account.
  • Google: after you continue with Google, your account email and storage quota (total, Drive, Drive trash, Gmail and Photos combined). OmniStore uses the Google scope drive.appdata only to read this quota. It does not open, read, change or delete your files, mail or photos.

Where it goes

Nowhere. All data stays on your device. OmniStore has no server and no analytics. Your Google sign-in token is stored in your operating system's credential store. Your Apple password is entered on Apple's page only and is never seen by OmniStore.

If you install OmniStore on several devices, each one writes a small storage summary (device name, model, disk sizes and free space) to your own iCloud Drive or OneDrive folder so your other devices can show it.

Google user data

OmniStore's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is never sold, shared, used for advertising or used to train models.

Removing your data

Use Sign out in OmniStore to remove the Google token and the iCloud session. Uninstalling OmniStore removes everything it stored. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
