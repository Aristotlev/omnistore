OmniStore

All your storage in one place. See your iCloud and Google plans, how full they are, what fills them, and what's on every device you own.

Download

Free. Installed copies update themselves.

Download for Windows Download for Mac

Windows 10 and 11. macOS 11 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.

First launch. Windows: if you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. Mac: drag OmniStore to Applications; if macOS can't verify the developer, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

What you get

Your plans

Your iCloud+ and Google One plan, its price, free space and how far over you are.

What fills iCloud

Photos, videos, screenshots and which apps saved them. iCloud Drive folders and Recently Deleted.

Every device

PC, Mac, iPhone and iPad on your account. Nothing to install on your iPhone or iPad.

What to fix first

Clear recommendations with a button that does it, from emptying trash to a cheaper plan.

Signing in

  • Apple: sign in on Apple's own page with a passkey or by approving on your iPhone. OmniStore never sees your password.
  • Google: click Continue with Google and pick your account. OmniStore only reads storage numbers.
  • Everything stays on your computer. No accounts, no servers, no analytics.
